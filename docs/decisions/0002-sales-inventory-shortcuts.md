# 0002 — Cash sales, sellable inventory and interface shortcuts

Status: the owner approved sales, inventory and shortcuts on 2026-10-26; the details below are documented implementation choices. Source: the owner's request, the sketch with products on the left and the ticket on the right, and confirmation of the existing Daftar application. Implementation: version 1.10.0, account schema 8.

## Decision and rationale

- Add sales, inventory and sales-history screens inside the existing local application, using Arabic and Daftar's calm color palette. The top bar appears on every screen and has six slots, any of which can be hidden or linked to a main screen in settings. Text remains Arabic; the product/ticket column order follows the sketch.
- Sellable products are separate from the supplier-purchase catalog. Ingredients may be purchased by the carton and meals sold, so no unspecified recipe or conversion factor is assumed. Receiving prepared products is an actual restock; a quantity movement alone creates neither an expense nor a debt. This version does not automatically deduct recipe ingredients.
- Quantity is tracked in each product's unit, to three decimal places, using integer thousandths. A meal can be created without quantity tracking. Once movements exist, the UI keeps tracking selection and unit fixed. Products are deactivated rather than having their history deleted.
- Sales are cash-only: lines, prices, a fixed-amount discount, cash received and customer change. The collected value is the total after discount, not cash received before returning change. The receipt preserves the name, unit and price at the time of sale. This version has no credit sales or electronic payments.
- Saving the sale and deducting inventory is one operation through the existing validation and atomic save. Negative inventory is prevented at every historical date, not only in the final balance. A failed save preserves the ticket for retry.
- Sales history filters by date, number and product, and exports a PDF receipt from saved data. Voiding applies to the whole sale with a reason and timestamp, without deletion; it restores inventory and excludes the value on the original sale date. Returning cash to the customer is a physical action performed by the user; the void button records its effect. This version implements neither partial returns nor moving the void to another day.
- Sales revenue belongs to the daily account; it is not automatically added to the cashbox because the daily deposit is recorded later. The remaining-cash snapshot stores the sales total at that point, and only the subsequent sales difference is added. Sales preceding entry of remaining cash are treated as included in the actual entered amount, preventing duplicate cash. The manually entered overall balance remains fixed under the earlier rules. Moving a day's expense records does not move sales or inventory; when sales differ, the remaining-cash snapshot is reset to preserve the actual remaining amount.
- Do not create illustrative products or opening balances in restaurant accounts. Screen and PDF experiments use only an isolated folder under `dist/commerce-qa/`.

Behavior reference: `app/ui/model.js`, `commerce.js` and `commerce.css`; verification evidence is in the [work log](../log/2026-10-26-sales-inventory.md).
